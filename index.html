About Yeko Empire Petroleum Distribution Limited

Yeko Empire Downstream Logistics Co, operating as Yeko Empire Petroleum Distribution Limited, is a leading player in Nigeria's downstream oil and gas sector. Based in Abuja, we specialize in the safe and efficient distribution of petroleum products across the Federal Capital Territory and beyond.

Our operations are guided by international best practices and a deep understanding of the Nigerian energy landscape. With expertise in energy trading and logistics management, we bridge the gap between refineries and end-users, ensuring that quality petroleum products reach their destinations on time, every time.

We pride ourselves on building lasting relationships with our clients through transparency, reliability, and unwavering commitment to safety standards. Our team of experienced professionals works round the clock to maintain the highest levels of operational excellence in every aspect of our business.

Comprehensive Downstream Solutions

Petroleum Product Distribution

Reliable supply and distribution of premium motor spirit (PMS), automotive gas oil (AGO), and dual-purpose kerosene (DPK) to retail outlets, industrial clients, and commercial users across Nigeria.

Energy Trading

Expert energy trading services leveraging market intelligence and strategic partnerships to optimize supply chains and deliver competitive pricing for bulk petroleum purchases.

Logistics Management

End-to-end logistics solutions including transportation, storage, and delivery coordination, ensuring your petroleum products arrive safely and on schedule.

Bulk Supply Solutions

Customized bulk fuel supply arrangements for corporate clients, construction companies, manufacturing facilities, and government institutions with flexible payment terms.

Consultancy Services

Strategic advisory services on downstream operations, regulatory compliance, and supply chain optimization for businesses in the energy sector.
